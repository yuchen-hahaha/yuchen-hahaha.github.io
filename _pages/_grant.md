## Taiwan Ministry of Science and Technology Grant 107-2314-B-182A-145-MY2

**Role:** Co-Principal Investigator  
**Period:** August 2018–July 2019

**Project:** The role of cardiorespiratory coupling in patients with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.

This project focused on quantifying cardiorespiratory coupling in patients with COPD and evaluating whether these physiological measures could help predict hospitalization.

## Taiwan Ministry of Science and Technology Grant 110-2314-B-182A-141-MY3

**Role:** Co-Principal Investigator; lead author of the proposal  
**Period:** August 2021–December 2025

**Project:** The role of cardiorespiratory coupling in patients with obstructive sleep apnea.

This project investigated the use of cardiorespiratory coupling for screening and diagnosis of obstructive sleep apnea, development of a home-screening approach, and prediction of cardiovascular outcomes.
